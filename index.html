faq

QUESTIONS BUSINESS OWNERS ASK

How long does a website take to build?

Most business websites take one to three weeks depending on scope. Landing pages can be faster; e-commerce and custom builds take longer. You'll get a timeline before work starts.

Can you build a website for my business?

Yes. I've built sites for consultancies, restaurants, schools, stores and personal brands. Tell me what your business does and I'll recommend the right approach.

Will my website work on phones?

Every site is built mobile-first, since most visitors will land on a phone. It's tested across screen sizes before launch.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. Depending on the package, you can request updates directly, or I can set up simple, editable sections for you.

Can you connect WhatsApp to my website?

Yes, this is standard on every build. Visitors can tap a button and start chatting with you directly, no extra steps.

Can you help me put my website online?

Yes. I'll guide you through hosting and domain setup, or handle deployment for you so your site goes live smoothly.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope and features. Starter, Business and Custom packages give a rough idea, but every quote is based on what your business actually needs.
